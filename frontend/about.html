We work out the cost before we suggest the trip.

TripMind is a small team in India building a trip planner that starts from your budget, not from a list of pretty places.

Why we started

Most trip planning in India begins the same way. Someone suggests a place, it looks lovely, and only after an evening of comparing trains, flights and hotel tabs do you find out it costs twice what you had in mind, or that you'd spend two of your four days getting there.

We wanted to flip that. Tell us where you're leaving from, how many days you have and what you can spend, and we'll only show you trips where the numbers already work.

How the planner decides

For every destination we consider, we cost the whole journey: the sensible way to get there for the distance (road, train or flight), somewhere to stay for your group, food, local transport and entry fees, adjusted for the month you're travelling in.

If the travel time eats too much of a short trip, or the total goes past your budget, the place is dropped. What's left is ranked by what you told us you wanted. Every figure is an estimate, and we're open about how the estimates are made.

What we don't do

We don't take bookings or commissions that change what you pay. We don't sell your data. We don't nudge you towards a pricier hotel. If nothing fits your budget, we say so and show you what would need to change.

The planner is free. The one thing we charge for is expert planning, where a person writes your day-by-day plan, and you pay only after we've confirmed we can do it.

Who we are

We're a small team. TripMind started in Indore and is built and run in India, by people who know what it's like to plan a family trip around a fixed budget and a few days of leave.

We're early, and the planner gets better each time someone tells us where it got something wrong. If a cost looked off or a route made no sense, write to us. We read everything.
