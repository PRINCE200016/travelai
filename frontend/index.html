Straight answers

How accurate are the prices?

They're planning estimates, typically within 10–20% of what you'll pay if you book a few weeks ahead. We use current fare ranges for each kind of transport, hotel rates for the comfort level you pick, and add seasonal surcharges for peak months. Last-minute bookings, festivals and long weekends can cost more.

Do you book anything?

No. TripMind plans; you book wherever you like. We link to Booking.com, IRCTC and flight search with your destination filled in, and none of those links change the price you pay.

What if nothing fits my budget?

We tell you plainly and show the closest options with exactly how much more they'd need, or how many fewer days would make them work. We never quietly change your budget or dates.

Which cities can I start from?

Over 70 cities across India are mapped precisely. If yours isn't one of them, we estimate from typical distances and tell you so on the results page.

Is my information shared?

No. Searches are used to produce your plan and to improve recommendations. We don't sell data. See our privacy policy.
