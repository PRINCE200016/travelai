What TripMind keeps in your browser, and what changes if you allow analytics.

Last updated: 14 November 2026

Essential storage

TripMind itself doesn't set advertising or tracking cookies. To work, the site keeps a few small items in your browser's local storage. They stay on your device, and only the sign-in token is ever sent to our server, to show that you're signed in.

NameWhat it's forHow long
userKeeps you signed in: your name, email and a sign-in token.Until you sign out
tm_saved_tripsTrips you've saved on this device.Until you remove them or clear browser data
tm_last_queryYour most recent search, so the planner can be refilled for you.Until replaced or cleared
tm_consentYour answer to the cookie notice, so we don't ask again.Until cleared

The planner may also keep the results of your current search in session storage while the tab is open. It's removed when you close the tab.

Analytics cookies, only if you allow them

If you click "Allow analytics" on the cookie notice, we load Google Analytics. It sets its own cookies to count visits and tell one visit from another, and we ask it to anonymise your IP address. We use the numbers to see which pages are used and where the planner confuses people. If you click "Essential only", or ignore the notice, Google Analytics is never loaded and none of these cookies are set.

NameSet byHow long
_gaGoogle Analytics, to distinguish visitorsUp to 2 years
_ga_<id>Google Analytics, to keep track of a visitUp to 2 years

Changing your choice

You can change your answer whenever you like. The notice will appear again so you can choose.

If you'd allowed analytics and now switch it off, Google's cookies already in your browser stay until they expire or you clear them in your browser settings. Clearing all site data for TripMind also removes your saved trips and signs you out.
