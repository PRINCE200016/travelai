Privacy policy

What we collect when you use TripMind, why we need it, and how to have it removed.

Last updated: 14 November 2026

Overview Privacy Terms Cookies How our estimates work

The short version

  • To plan a trip, the details you enter are sent to our server so we can work out costs. We keep them to improve the planner.
  • If you're signed in, your searches are linked to your account so you can see them again.
  • Saved trips stay in your browser. We don't receive them.
  • Google Analytics only loads if you click "Allow analytics".
  • We don't sell your data and we don't show ads.

What we collect and why

When you plan a trip

The planner sends what you enter to our server: where you're leaving from, when, for how many days, how many travellers, your budget and the kind of trip you'd like. We need these to calculate the plans. We store the searches so we can spot mistakes in our estimates and see which places people are looking for. If you're not signed in, a search isn't tied to your name or email.

Like any website, our server also receives technical details with each request, such as your IP address and browser type. We use these to keep the service running and to stop abuse.

If you create an account

We store your name, email address and a password. The password is stored as a one-way hash, so we can't read it. While you're signed in, your searches are linked to your account so you can return to them.

Saved trips

When you save a trip, it's kept in your browser's local storage on that device. It isn't sent to us. Clearing your browser data, or using a different browser or device, means you won't see it.

Expert-planning requests

When you ask for expert planning, we store your name, email, phone number, destination, dates, number of travellers, budget, preferences and any notes. We use them to plan your trip, contact you about it and arrange payment.

Messages to us

If you use the contact form, we store your name, email, phone number if you give one, and your message, so that we can reply. The same applies if you email or WhatsApp us.

Analytics, only with your permission

We use Google Analytics to understand which pages are used and where people get stuck. It doesn't load until you click "Allow analytics" on the cookie notice. If you choose "Essential only", it never loads. When it does run, we ask Google to anonymise your IP address. See the cookie policy for details and to change your choice.

What we don't do

We don't sell or rent personal data. We don't share it with advertisers, and we don't use it for advertising. We don't build profiles of you beyond the searches linked to your own account.

Who else handles your data

A few services help us run TripMind, and they process data only on our behalf:

  • Hosting providers that run our website, servers and database.
  • Our email provider, which we use to reply to you and send expert plans.
  • EmailJS, which forwards expert-planning requests (name, contact details and trip details) to our team's inbox.
  • Google Analytics, only if you've allowed analytics.
  • Google's Gemini service, which suggests destinations for your search. It receives your trip details (starting town, month, number of days, group size, budget, trip style and anything you write in "describe your dream trip"), never your name, email or phone number. It is not used to calculate prices.
  • Open-Meteo, Wikipedia and OpenStreetMap, which we use to confirm suggested places and look up their weather. They receive place names and map coordinates only, plus your starting town's name if it isn't one we already know.

When you follow a link from TripMind to a booking site such as IRCTC or a hotel site, that site's own privacy policy applies.

We may disclose information if Indian law requires it, for example in response to a valid order from a court or authority.

How long we keep it

Account details are kept while your account exists. Expert-planning requests and messages are kept as long as we need them to deal with your request and keep sensible records. Search records are kept to improve the planner. You can ask us to delete any of it at any time.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us for a copy of the personal data we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, including your account and the searches linked to it. Write to arjunrajawat28@gmail.com from the email address you used with us. We'll respond within 30 days, and usually much sooner. These rights reflect India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

You can withdraw permission for analytics at any time from the cookie policy page.

Keeping it safe

The site and our server use HTTPS. Passwords are hashed. Access to stored requests and messages is limited to the people who answer them. No system is perfectly secure, so if we ever learn of a breach that affects you, we'll tell you.

Children

TripMind is meant for adults planning trips. Please don't create an account or send a request if you're under 18.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle data, we'll update this page and the date at the top. For significant changes affecting account holders, we'll also email you.
