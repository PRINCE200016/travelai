Questions about paying

Something else? Ask us.

Is the planner really free?

Yes. There's no trial, no limit on searches and no paid tier hiding the useful parts. The cost breakdown you see is the same for everyone.

How does paying for expert planning work?

Send the request form with your destination, dates and budget. We check that we can plan it well and reply to confirm, with how to pay. You pay ₹199 only after that, and the clock on the 24-hour delivery starts once payment is received.

What if you can't plan my trip?

We tell you, and you pay nothing. That can happen with very remote places, dates that are too close, or a budget that can't cover the basics. We'll say which.

Can I get a refund?

If your plan doesn't reach you within 24 hours of payment, you get the full ₹199 back. Once it's delivered the fee isn't refundable, but if something in it is wrong, tell us and we'll fix it. The details are in our terms.

Does TripMind book hotels or tickets?

No. We plan and you book wherever you prefer. Expert plans include links and names so you can book directly, and we don't add anything to the price you pay.
